# HAA-D-3 — The Neural Odyssey

> An interdisciplinary research, systems-engineering and creative-production program: from scientific evidence and mathematical models to interactive explanation and cinematic media.

**Project codename:** HAA-D-3  
**Narrative / publication title:** The Neural Odyssey  
**Baseline:** 0.1.0 — Documentation and prototype foundation  
**Status:** Research planning; first illustrative vertical slice added on a work branch  
**Repository:** https://github.com/metsam-mastem/HAA-D-3

## Mission

Build a traceable system to research, model, simulate, explain, visualize and turn selected scientific/engineering findings into high-quality audiovisual experiences. The program connects neuroscience, mathematics, physics, computer science, scientific visualization, interaction design, animation, cinematography and original sound design.

This repository does **not** claim a validated novel neural architecture, measured performance gain, or biologically accurate simulation.

## Three-chapter creative frame

1. **Chapter I — The Genesis / پیدایش:** foundations, questions, mathematical language and concept formation.
2. **Chapter II — The Convergence / همگرایی:** architecture, relationships, feedback, modeling and the research-to-scene bridge.
3. **Chapter III — The Emergence / ظهور:** validation, synthesis, visual storytelling and cinematic presentation.

Music references in earlier planning notes are creative mood references only. No copyrighted audio, lyrics or media are redistributed. Final score direction, sequencing and synchronization require a separate rights-aware brief.

## Operating model

HAA-D-3 has two engines and one bridge:

- **Research Engine:** sources, claims, datasets, models, experiments, validation and benchmarks.
- **Creative Engine:** scene design, storyboard, shots, camera, lighting, animation, sound and rendering.
- **Knowledge & Scene Bridge:** versioned, typed transformation from approved evidence/model outputs to scene specifications.

Every meaningful claim and visual must distinguish OBSERVED, REPRODUCED, MODEL_OUTPUT, HYPOTHESIS, INTERPRETATION, METAPHOR and UNKNOWN.

## Vertical Slice 01 — Neural Emergence

A lightweight browser scene explores a three-act visual sequence: Genesis → Convergence → Emergence. It uses deterministic synthetic nodes and links, chapter controls, play/pause and a timeline scrubber.

**Scientific status:** illustrative synthetic graph only. It is not a neural simulation, connectome, anatomical reconstruction or measured dataset. Its purpose is to test visual direction and the future scene-to-render handoff.

Run by opening `demo/neural-odyssey/index.html` in a modern browser or serving the repository root with a static HTTP server.

## Research position

- Spatial embedding is a research direction; 3D placement alone is not evidence of improved intelligence.
- Feedback mechanisms require objectives, stability analysis and ablation benchmarks.
- Foundation models can serve as baselines; custom spatial/feedback systems must be bounded and tested.
- Sparse topology, low-rank methods, quantization and selective execution are candidates, not promised gains.
- Scientific visuals must remain faithful to sources and distinguish measurement from metaphor.

## Governance and status

Preserve existing work. Prefer additive feature branches, explicit contracts, reproducible tests and evidence-backed decisions. Use PASS / FAIL / NOT RUN / BLOCKED. Review licenses, provenance, data privacy and media rights before reuse.

Research, benchmarks, biological validation, full 3D production and final cinematic soundtrack are not complete. Do not claim completion without evidence.
