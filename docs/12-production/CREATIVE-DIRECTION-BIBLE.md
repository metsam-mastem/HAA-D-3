# Creative Direction Bible — The Neural Odyssey

## Identity
Core feeling: awe through precision. Genre language: scientific epic, minimal cyberpunk and abstract engineering cinema. Tone: serious, restrained, deep, monumental and curious. Avoid generic neon overload, random HUD decoration, pseudo-scientific labels and particle clutter.

## Visual grammar
- Darkness is a compositional tool.
- Light reveals structure and marks meaningful transitions.
- Geometry communicates hierarchy, relation, tension or change.
- Motion expresses cause, propagation, convergence or transformation.
- Camera movement reveals information or changes scale.
- Every overlay has a narrative or analytical function.

## Palette and materials
Obsidian, graphite and cold white; restrained cyan and steel blue; limited amber/coral accent. Matte black, smoked glass, brushed metal and translucent volumetric layers.

## Cinematography
Slow controlled dolly, orbital reveal, macro-to-system scale transitions, negative-space framing and precise cuts. Avoid constant motion. Reserve acceleration for structural change.

## Sound
Original score concept: low sustained harmonic bed, granular/metallic textures, sparse pulses, evolving strings/brass-like layers and deliberate silence. Build an energy curve across acts. Keep stems separable and rights documented.

## Narrative discipline
Every sequence needs a question, a visible transformation, a reason for each shot, a moment of tension and a resolution that does not exceed the evidence.

## Chapter arc
Genesis: origin, isolation, question.  
Convergence: interaction, structure, tension, transformation.  
Emergence: organization, consequence and open horizon.
