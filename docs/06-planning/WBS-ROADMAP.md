# WBS & Roadmap — Planning Baseline

## Work Breakdown Structure
0 HAA-D-3 The Neural Odyssey
1 Program definition: problem, mission, scope, stakeholder, metrics.
2 Knowledge universe: taxonomy, dependency graph, glossary, source/evidence/hypothesis ledgers.
3 Scientific research: math; model families; spatial topology; feedback/stability; memory/continual learning.
4 Systems research: requirements, interfaces, CI, hardware/resource, security, license/provenance.
5 Architecture: alternatives, planes, contracts, ADRs, integration/failure boundaries.
6 ES: requirements, schema/API, verification matrix, review/baseline.
7 Vertical slice: fixtures, validator, renderer, receipt, CLI, tests, CI.
8 Neural experiments: task/data rights, baseline, experimental module, ablations/profiling.
9 Creative: design system, episode cards, storyboard, code/prompt scene plans, animation, audio rights.
10 Publication: technical report, figures, sources, series/presentation, release review.
11 Governance: risk/decision/change logs, quality gates, maintenance.

## Phases and gates
P0 Charter — scope and success criteria review.
P1 Knowledge map — branches, prerequisites, sources and gaps.
P2 Deep research — evidence summaries and limitations.
P3 Architecture feasibility — alternatives and trade-offs.
P4 ES — interfaces and verification traceability.
P5 Knowledge graph slice — tests/CI and visual review.
P6 Neural task — baseline/ablations/reproducible results.
P7 Publication — claims and figures source-linked.
P8 Creative series — scientific fidelity and music rights check.

## Parallel streams
Research; architecture/ES; graph prototype; benchmark/resources; visual/cinema/music; governance. Connect them with source, requirement, ADR and artifact IDs.

## Indicative ranges (solo-led, not commitments)
P0 1 week; P1 2–4 weeks; P2 4–8; P3 2–4; P4 3–6; P5 1–3 days; P6 1–3+ weeks; P7 2–4 weeks; P8 variable. Overlap possible; revise after workload/hardware is known.

## Done definition
Artifact exists, scope/version identified, test/review evidence attached, risks/open items listed, status set, index updated.
