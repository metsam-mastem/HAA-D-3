# Three Chapters & Series Bible
**Series:** The Neural Odyssey / اودیسهٔ هوش · **Status:** concept v0.1

## Premise
A journey from a raw hypothesis about spatial modular neural computation to testable engineering and a visual account of emergence. The three-act arc can support a complete educational/creative season; production remains a proposal.
