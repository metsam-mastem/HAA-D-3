# Vertical Slice 01 — Neural Emergence

**Status:** Prototype specification and illustrative browser demo  
**Target:** 45–60 second cinematic proof-of-direction  
**Scientific status:** Synthetic illustration only; not a biological model.

## Objective
Prove that a structured scene can drive a coherent visual sequence with a serious, minimal, futuristic tone while distinguishing scientific evidence from artistic metaphor.

## Story premise
A silent field of isolated signals begins to form relationships. Local interactions create a larger pattern. The system transitions from fragmentation to coordinated motion, then resolves into a stable visual structure. This represents the idea of emergence, not a measured claim about biological intelligence.

## Three acts
1. Genesis (0–15s): darkness, sparse points, isolated pulses and restrained typography.
2. Convergence (15–38s): connections appear, waves travel, geometry becomes organized.
3. Emergence (38–55s): the network resolves into a coherent form; scale changes and final title.

## Visual direction
Near-black, graphite, cold white and restrained cyan; negative space; asymmetry; controlled depth; deliberate scale changes; slow camera drift; precise pulses; modern typography. Serious, monumental and cyberpunk-adjacent, not generic neon overload.

## Sound direction
Original low-frequency drone, sparse metallic textures, evolving orchestral tension and deliberate silence. No copyrighted reference track is bundled.

## Data truth
The demo uses deterministic synthetic nodes and edges. It has no biological measurements, neuron morphology or fitted neural dynamics. All visuals are METAPHOR / SYNTHETIC.

## Acceptance criteria
- Runs from a local static server without API keys or external services.
- Three acts can be selected and played.
- Pause/play and progress controls work.
- Visual state is deterministic for the same scene version.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected.
- Labels identify the sequence as illustrative.
- No unsupported scientific statement appears.
- No copyrighted audio or imagery is bundled.
- Human review records clarity, pacing, legibility and emotional coherence.

## Out of scope
No novel neural architecture claim, biological realism, experimental validation, full 3D render, voiceover, final soundtrack or commercial release.
