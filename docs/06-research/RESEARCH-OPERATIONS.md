# Research Operations

## Research intake
Every research task begins with a question, intended decision or learning outcome, scope, date window, disciplines, exclusion criteria and expected deliverable.

## Source quality tiers
- Primary research and original datasets.
- Systematic reviews, meta-analyses and authoritative methods papers.
- Peer-reviewed reviews and reference works.
- Official software documentation and source repositories.
- Preprints, technical reports and working papers.
- Community discussions and opinion (discovery only unless independently verified).

These are not automatic truth rankings; relevance and methods still require review.

## Research record
Capture: source_id, exact title, authors/organization, publication date, access date, DOI/URL, version, source type, license, relevant passage/page/section, claims supported, caveats and reviewer.

## Synthesis
Separate direct source statements, reproduced results, interpretation, open questions and creative analogies. Record disagreement. Avoid citation laundering: follow a citation to the original source where practical.

## Reproducibility
For computational findings record code revision, environment, dependency lock, dataset version, preprocessing, random seed, parameters, command, logs, metrics and artifact checksums.

## Research-to-production gate
No finding enters a literal/analytical scene until its evidence class, units, source, method, uncertainty and visual transform are documented. Metaphors may enter only with an explicit metaphor label.

## Deliverables
Research brief, claim ledger, dataset card, model card, experiment record, reproducibility bundle, scene mapping and unresolved questions.
