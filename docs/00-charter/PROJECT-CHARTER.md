# Project Charter — HAA-D-3 / The Neural Odyssey
**ID:** HAA-D-3-CH-001 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Status:** Draft baseline

## Purpose
Govern an evidence-led research and engineering program exploring hybrid neural systems and a traceable knowledge-to-visualization pipeline.

## Problem
Spatial neuron placement, modular computation, denoising, and multi-loop feedback are broad ideas. Without disciplined research they risk being conflated, incompatibly combined, unstable, resource-heavy, or supported by weak claims.

## Mission & vision
Mission: turn interdisciplinary research into testable architecture choices and reproducible artifacts. Vision: a living knowledge system linking sources, mathematical models, decisions, requirements, code, results, and visual storytelling.

## Objectives
- Build a multidimensional taxonomy with prerequisites.
- Maintain source, evidence, hypothesis, contradiction, and decision ledgers.
- Compare foundation-model baselines with bounded custom modules.
- Specify spatial graph, feedback, and adapter interfaces.
- Deliver a small runnable vertical slice.
- Establish benchmark and resource accounting before performance claims.
- Develop three chapters and an episodic presentation framework.

## In scope
Mathematics; neural architecture; pretrained models; spatial graphs; feedback/learning/control; systems and software engineering; compute profiling; knowledge engineering; scientific visualization; animation/code/prompt pipeline; research and governance.

## Out of scope for initial baseline
Training a large foundation model from scratch; AGI claims; full brain simulation; uncontrolled multi-agent loops; production deployment; unlicensed third-party code/media reuse; publishing soundtrack audio.

## Deliverables
Charter, manifesto, taxonomy, research protocol, sources, architecture concept, draft ES, WBS, risks, landscape, benchmark plan, series bible, vertical-slice code/tests, animation pipeline, later research synthesis.

## Assumptions & constraints
Solo-led AI-assisted work is assumed; schedules are ranges. Open-source does not imply license compatibility. Public repo excludes secrets, private media, model weights, personal data, copyrighted audio. Novelty and performance require literature and experiment.

## Governance
Architecture changes require ADRs; claims carry evidence/status; features require acceptance criteria. Status vocabulary: PASS, FAIL, NOT RUN, BLOCKED, DRAFT, SOURCE-CLAIM.

## Success criteria
Traceable claims; explicit taxonomy and gaps; verifiable ES; reproducible slice; quality/resource metrics; scientifically faithful visuals; documented rights and provenance.
